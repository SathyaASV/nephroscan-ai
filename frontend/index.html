Why NephroScan AI?

Reviewing medical images is time-consuming and specialist-dependent. NephroScan AI explores how an explainable, transparent decision-support layer could sit alongside a radiologist's workflow — surfacing quality checks, candidate findings, model confidence, and structured reporting, while always deferring the actual diagnosis to a qualified clinician.

Responsible AI Disclaimer
NephroScan AI is an educational AI-assisted medical imaging prototype. Its predictions are not a medical diagnosis and should not replace evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional. Model performance depends on training data, image quality, imaging modality, and clinical context. This system has not been established as a clinically validated diagnostic device. Review generated findings with qualified clinical judgment.
